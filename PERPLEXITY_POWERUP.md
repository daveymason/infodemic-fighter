## What It Does

The Perplexity Power-Up uses Sonar AI to provide instant transparency about media funding:

- **Ownership Structure**: Who owns and controls the media outlet
- **Funding Sources**: Primary revenue streams and financial backers  
- **Corporate Ties**: Business relationships and potential conflicts of interest
- **Risk Assessment**: How funding might influence editorial decisions

## Why It Matters

Financial transparency is crucial for media literacy. Understanding who pays for journalism helps users:
- Identify potential conflicts of interest
- Recognize editorial independence (or lack thereof)
- Make informed decisions about source credibility
- Combat financially-motivated misinformation

*Powered by Perplexity's Sonar API for real-time, comprehensive media analysis.*
